Every genome below was read from NCBI GenBank and scanned gene by gene. A genome is flagged when it carries an integrase, excisionase, CI-like repressor, site-specific recombinase or transposase (lysogeny), or a toxin or virulence term. Select any row to see the genes that triggered it.

Keyword screen of GenBank product annotations — a first-pass filter, not a safety certificate. Confirmation against CARD and VFDB is the next step.

The designer picks phages one at a time, each time taking the candidate least similar to everything already chosen, measured on receptor-binding proteins and whole genomes. Phages stopped by the gate are never offered.

Pairwise distance within the cocktail

1.000 means no shared receptor-protein or genome k-mers. Higher is better: the phages are less likely to fail together.

This is the part a hospital laboratory uses. Paste a plate matrix — one row per phage, one column per clinical isolate, 1 where the phage cleared the strain and 0 where it did not — and the tool returns the smallest cocktail covering the most isolates.
